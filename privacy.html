Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ORHAR. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy, securing your spiritual reflections, and providing a transparent understanding of our data practices in full compliance with global privacy regulations including the GDPR (Europe) and CCPA/CPRA (California).

2. Information We Collect

We operate on the principle of data minimization. We collect only the information that is strictly essential for providing and improving our services.

A. Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, password (encrypted), and display name.
  • User-Generated Content: We collect the content you create within the app, such as the text and tags of your personal notes, prayers, and reflections, to sync them across your devices.
  • Communications: If you contact us directly, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage & Preference Data: We collect information about your activity to preserve your experience, such as your quiz progress, selected UI theme, font size, and chosen Bible version.
  • Device Information: We collect basic, non-identifying telemetry (operating system, device type, crash logs) to diagnose bugs and improve compatibility.
  • Guest Account Identifiers: For users who do not create an email account, we generate a unique device identifier to maintain your session and save your reading progress locally. We do not use this for cross-app tracking or advertising.

3. Third-Party Sub-Processors

To operate ORHAR, we share strictly necessary data with industry-leading service providers who adhere to the highest security standards. We do not sell your personal data.

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication, cloud database hosting (Firestore), and crash reporting. Your notes and account data are stored securely on Firebase servers.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage premium subscriptions. We do not collect or store your payment information (e.g., credit cards). Financial transactions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). RevenueCat only securely transmits your subscription status (Free/Premium) to our app.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create, maintain, and secure your account.
  • To sync your personal notes, highlights, and preferences across multiple devices.
  • To verify premium subscription status and unlock features.
  • To analyze app performance, fix crashes, and improve the user experience.
  • To respond to customer support inquiries and send critical account security notices. We do not send marketing emails without explicit opt-in consent.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you possess comprehensive rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Right to Opt-Out: While we do not sell data, California residents have the right to opt-out of future data sales.

6. Data Deletion Policy

You retain total control over your spiritual journal and account data. You may delete your data in two ways:

  • In-App Deletion: You can instantly delete individual notes, reset quiz progress, or delete your entire account directly from the "Settings" menu within the ORHAR app.
  • Email Request: You may submit a data deletion request by emailing us from the address associated with your account. We will process and confirm the permanent deletion of your data within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

ORHAR is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete that data from our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or app features. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notice or email prior to the change becoming effective.
