Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 1, 2026

Welcome to ORHAR. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we handle your data. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data when using the ORHAR mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information essential to providing and improving your spiritual journey within the app.

  • Account Information: If you create an account, we collect your email address and display name.
  • User-Generated Content: We securely store data you create, such as personal notes, tags, highlights, and prayers, so they sync across your devices.
  • Usage & Device Data: We automatically collect app settings (theme, font size, selected Bible version), quiz progress, and basic device diagnostics (OS, device model) to fix bugs.
  • Guest Account ID: For users who do not sign in, we generate an anonymous device identifier to save reading progress and preferences locally. This is not used for tracking or advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to operate and improve ORHAR:

  • To sync your notes, highlights, and preferences across devices.
  • To manage your premium subscription status and unlock features.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve application performance.
  • To respond to your support requests. We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted infrastructure partners required to run the app:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure cloud hosting, database storage (Firestore), and user authentication.
  • RevenueCat: Used to manage subscription statuses. We do not collect or see your credit card information; all payments are processed directly by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.

4. Data Security

Your data is encrypted both in transit (via HTTPS) and at rest on Firebase servers using industry-standard AES-256 encryption. While we implement robust security measures, no digital transmission is 100% secure.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under global privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectify any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erase your data and close your account entirely.

Data Deletion Request

You can delete your data (notes, history) or your entire account directly inside the ORHAR app settings. Alternatively, you may email us at contact@orhar.com with the subject "Account Deletion". We will process your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ORHAR is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete it.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@orhar.com.

Vous pouvez supprimer vos données ou votre compte directement dans les paramètres de l'application ORHAR. Vous pouvez également nous envoyer un e-mail à contact@orhar.com avec pour objet "Suppression de compte". Nous traiterons votre demande sous 30 jours.
